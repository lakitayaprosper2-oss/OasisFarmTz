About OasisFarmTz

OasisFarmTz was founded with a simple mission: to provide the people of Tanzania with fresh, healthy, and affordable farm produce. Nestled in the fertile coastal plains of Bagamoyo in the Pwani Region, our farm has been nurturing the land and its people for years.

We believe that great food starts with healthy soil. That's why we use organic farming practices that enrich the earth naturally, without the use of harmful pesticides or synthetic fertilizers. Our animals are raised free-range, given space and care that reflects our respect for all living things.

OasisFarmTz is more than just a farm – it's a community. We work closely with local farmers, training them in sustainable techniques and providing fair wages. We also run educational programs for young people interested in agriculture, because we believe the future of Tanzania's food security lies in empowering the next generation.

From our paddy fields to your plate, every grain, vegetable, and piece of meat that leaves OasisFarmTz carries with it the dedication and love of our entire team. We are proud to serve Tanzania, and we are committed to growing with our community.

Location: Bagamoyo, Pwani Region, Tanzania
Established: Serving the community with organic produce
Mission: Fresh, natural, and community-driven farming
